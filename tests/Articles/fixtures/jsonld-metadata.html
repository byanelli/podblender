The Long Road To The New Bridge

Engineers spent the better part of the morning walking the length of the proposed span, measuring the distance between the old pilings and the fresh survey stakes that now dot the muddy riverbank on both sides of the water.

The bridge has been promised to residents for the better part of a generation, and each successive administration has found a new reason to postpone the groundbreaking that everyone in the valley has long since stopped expecting.

On Tuesday, though, the mood was different. The contractors arrived with heavy equipment and a signed schedule, and by noon the first of the temporary access roads had been graded well enough to carry a loaded cement truck.

Local business owners, who have watched traffic detour miles out of their way for years, gathered near the water to watch the work begin and to trade the kind of cautious optimism that comes from having been disappointed many times before.
